2. **Absolute expiresAt** — extension moves `expiresAt` under caps; pass uses absolute time (deterministic).
3. **P0 never shed** — storage pressure may leave `degraded: true` at the P0 floor rather than dropping audit/security or mutating payloads.
4. **Deletion isolation** — `AUTHORITATIVE_KINDS` prefixes refused on delete; `kind` claim refused on ingest; store has no workflow maps.
